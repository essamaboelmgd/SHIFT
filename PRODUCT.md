# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS for the first implementation surface.

## Users

Founder-led businesses in Egypt and Arab markets, generally already operating and growing, evaluating whether SHIFT can bring their digital presence up to the level of the business.

## Product Purpose

SHIFT's website is its first proof of craft. The first surface should make the offer legible in seconds, communicate maturity and trust, and give a serious founder a clear path to start a project.

## Positioning

SHIFT builds websites and web solutions for businesses that have outgrown their current digital presence, turning the website from a brochure into a tool that moves the business forward.

## Operating Context

Arabic-first, RTL, dark-first marketing surface. The website is evaluated quickly on mobile and desktop by non-technical business owners. WhatsApp and a short project form will become primary conversion paths in later sections.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current public focus is websites and web solutions. Mobile apps, marketing, social, media, and future creative services are not positioned as the primary offer on this hero. Do not fabricate clients, testimonials, metrics, or case studies.

## Brand Commitments

Name: SHIFT. Umbrella direction: SHIFT Creative Solutions. Near black #0A0A0A, SHIFT Orange #FE5E0E, warm white type, Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic copy, Inter for English metadata. The supplied SHIFT wordmark and favicon are authoritative assets.

Approved Figma Orbit hero frames: desktop `68:7`, mobile `68:64`, in file `Uibom7vJup44tJ6VgXX3kG`. Real visual asset: `public/assets/shift-glass-field.png`. Supplied logo: `public/logo/wordmark-white.png`.

## Product Principles

1. The site itself must demonstrate the quality it sells.
2. Progress is the outcome; technology is the tool.
3. One clear offer beats a vague full-service agency message.
4. Motion and visual detail support comprehension rather than distract from it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-visible focus, clear Arabic copy, reduced-motion support, and an accessible mobile menu with an explicit expanded state.
